title: Cancellations, waiting time and no-shows
---
## Cancelling a ride
Cancelling is free within 2 minutes of a driver accepting your request. After 2 minutes, a $5.00 cancellation fee applies, because the driver is already on the way.

## When the cancellation fee is waived
We refund the cancellation fee automatically if the driver was more than 5 minutes later than the arrival time shown when you booked, or if the driver asked you to cancel.

## Waiting time and no-shows
Once the driver arrives, the first 2 minutes of waiting are free; after that, waiting time is charged at $0.35/min. If you do not show up within 5 minutes, the driver may cancel and a $5.00 no-show fee applies.

## Drivers who cancel
If drivers cancel your ride several times, you are never charged for their cancellations. Repeated driver cancellations can be reported in the app so we can review them.
