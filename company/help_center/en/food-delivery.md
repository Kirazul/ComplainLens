## Missing or wrong items
Report missing or wrong items within 24 hours of delivery. We refund the price of the missing items; for wrong items, a photo helps us resolve it faster.

## Late delivery
If an order arrives more than 20 minutes after the latest estimated time shown in the app, you receive a $5.00 LunaGo credit automatically.

## Order not received
If the courier marked your order as delivered but you did not receive it, tell us within 24 hours. We check the courier's GPS trace and refund the order when the delivery cannot be confirmed.

## Food quality
Restaurants are responsible for food preparation. Report quality problems with a photo; refunds for quality issues are reviewed case by case by our Billing team.
