title: App problems, account access and security
---
## The app is not working
Update LunaGo to the latest version, check your internet connection and restart the app. Known outages are listed on our status page. Rides affected by a confirmed outage are reviewed and refunded when you were charged for a service you did not receive.

## Cannot log in
Use "Forgot password" on the login screen. If you no longer have access to your email or phone number, our Account team verifies your identity and restores access within 24 hours.

## Hacked account or unauthorised charges
If you see rides you did not take, change your password immediately and tell us. We secure the account within 24 hours and refund confirmed unauthorised charges after our investigation.

## Account deactivation
Accounts can be deactivated for fraud or safety reasons. You may request a review of a deactivation once, by writing to support.
