## Reporting a driver
Rudeness, an unprofessional attitude, a dirty vehicle or a refused trip can be reported within 7 days of the trip. Each report is reviewed by our Driver Operations team within 48 hours.

## Longer route than expected
If a driver took a clearly longer route without your request, we compare the route with the suggested one. When the difference is confirmed, we adjust the fare to the price of the suggested route.

## Ratings
Your rating is anonymous. Drivers whose ratings stay below our standards receive coaching and, if nothing improves, can be removed from the platform.
