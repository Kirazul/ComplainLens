## In an emergency
If you are in danger or someone is hurt, call 911 first. Your safety matters more than any report to us.

## Reporting a safety concern
Dangerous driving, harassment, threats, an impaired driver, an accident or any situation where you felt unsafe can be reported in the app or by writing to support. Safety reports are handled by our dedicated Safety team, 24 hours a day.

## What happens next
A member of the Safety team contacts you within 1 hour. While we investigate, the driver's account can be paused. We never ask you to contact the driver yourself, and we do not share the outcome of disciplinary measures, but we tell you when the investigation is closed.

## Zero tolerance
Discrimination, harassment and violence lead to permanent removal from LunaGo.
