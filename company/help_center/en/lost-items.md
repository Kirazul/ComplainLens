## Reporting a lost item
Report a lost item within 30 days of the trip, in the app under Account → Trips → Report lost item, or by writing to support with the date and time of the trip.

## Getting it back
We contact the driver on your behalf; we never share personal phone numbers. If the driver has the item, you agree on a drop-off time. A $15.00 return fee is paid to the driver for their time.

## If the driver cannot be reached
If the driver does not answer within 24 hours, our Lost & Found team follows up directly and keeps you informed. Valuable items (phones, wallets, ID documents) are handled with priority.
