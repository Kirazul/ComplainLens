## How your fare is calculated
Every ride shows an upfront price before you book. It combines a base fare ($2.75), a per-kilometre rate ($0.95/km), a per-minute rate ($0.35/min) and a booking fee ($1.50). The minimum fare is $6.50.

## Surge pricing
When demand is high, prices are multiplied by a surge factor (for example ×1.4). The factor is always shown before you confirm the ride. You never pay a surge you did not see and accept.

## When the final price can differ from the upfront price
The final price can change only if you add a stop, change the destination, the trip takes more than 25% longer because of a route you requested, or tolls apply. Your receipt lists the reason for any change.

## Charged twice or an unexpected amount
A second line on your bank statement is often a temporary authorisation hold, not a charge. Holds disappear automatically within 3 to 5 business days. If you were really charged twice for one ride or order, we refund the duplicate to your original payment method; refunds take 5 to 10 business days to appear, depending on your bank.

## Receipts
A receipt is emailed after every ride and order, and is available in the app under Account → Trips.
